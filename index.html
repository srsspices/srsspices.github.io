About Us

Rooted in Flavour,
Built on Trust

SRS Spices is a MSME-registered manufacturing enterprise based in Palam Vihar, Gurugram. We grind and process premium quality spices in our own unit — giving us full control over purity, hygiene, and freshness from raw material to final pack.

Founded in October 2026, we serve retail customers directly through e-commerce and supply bulk orders to businesses, restaurants, and distributors across Haryana and NCR.
